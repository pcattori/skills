Glossary

  1. +Claim: A reservation that signals a giver intends to give a wishlist item.

Behavior

  1. −Restrict who can leave: Only the event organizer can leave an event.
  2. Claim visibility: Recipients can see who claimed an item for an event −as soon as it’s claimed +after the event ends.

    Why: This keeps the gift a surprise and still helps recipients write thank-you notes.

Scope

  1. +Claims can’t be transferred: Until: the Transfer a claim capability is specified.
